# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: February 2026**

Check Integrity ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Overview
Check Integrity is a utility app designed to help users verify the security status and integrity of their Android devices. We prioritize your privacy and do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or precise location data.

## 2. Information We Process
To provide integrity and root detection services, the App processes certain technical data. This data is non-identifiable and is used solely for the App's core functionality.

### A. Technical Device Data
The App analyzes technical details including:
- OS Version and Build Fingerprint.
- Hardware specifications (CPU, Manufacturer, Model).
- Root status indicators (e.g., presence of SU binaries, Busybox, or test-keys).
- Security environment status (e.g., SELinux status, presence of hooking frameworks or emulators).

### B. Google Play Integrity API
We use the **Google Play Integrity API** to verify that the App is running on a genuine Android device and has not been tampered with. This service may collect technical information as governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

### C. Backend Verification
Technical tokens generated by the Play Integrity API are sent to our secure backend server for validation. This communication is encrypted via HTTPS. No device identifiers or technical data are stored permanently on our servers after the verification process is complete.

## 3. Advertising and Third-Party Services
The App displays advertisements to support its development. These third-party services may collect data as governed by their respective policies:
- **AdMob (Google)**: Uses device identifiers to serve ads. [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Firebase (Google)**: Used for App Check and performance monitoring to improve app stability.

## 4. Data Security
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect the technical data processed during integrity checks. We do not transmit any personal data from your device.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion
- **Local Data**: Technical data stored in the App's local cache is deleted when the user clears the App's storage or uninstalls the App.
- **Server Data**: Since we do not collect personal identifiers, there is no user-specific data stored on our servers to delete. Technical tokens sent for verification are processed in memory and are not stored permanently.
- **Inquiries**: If you have any concerns regarding data processed by the App, you may contact us at support@arhdevice.com.

## 6. Children's Privacy
Our App does not collect any personal information and is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

## 8. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at: **support@arhdevice.com**
