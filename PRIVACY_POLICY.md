# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: February 2025**

Check Integrity is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information Collection
We do not collect personal identifiable information. The application uses Google Play Integrity API and Firebase App Check to verify the security and integrity of your device. These services may collect technical information such as:
- Device identifiers
- OS version
- Hardware details
- App installation status

## 2. Use of Information
The technical data collected is used solely for:
- Verifying device integrity
- Detecting rooting, hooking, or emulation
- Ensuring the app is running in a secure environment
- Preventing fraud and abuse

## 3. Third-Party Services
We use the following third-party services which have their own privacy policies:
- **Google Play Services**: [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Firebase**: [Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

## 4. Data Security
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect technical data processed during integrity checks. No data is stored permanently on our servers.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@arhdevice.com
