# Privacy Policy for Check Integrity

**Last Updated: February 23, 2024**

Check Integrity ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Check Integrity mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our security diagnostic service to you.

### A. Technical and Device Information
To perform security analysis (root detection, system integrity, and device authenticity), the App accesses technical device information, including:
*   Device model, manufacturer, and hardware specifications.
*   Android OS version and build fingerprint.
*   System properties and bootloader status.
*   Detection of system modification tools (e.g., Magisk, Xposed, KernelSU).

### B. Sensitive Permissions Disclosure (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES)
Our App uses the `QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES` permission. 
*   **Purpose:** This is a **core feature** of the App used strictly for security purposes to detect potentially harmful applications, rooting frameworks, and hooking environments that could compromise the integrity of your device.
*   **Data Usage:** We only check for the presence of known security-related package names. We **do not** collect, store, or share a full list of your installed applications.
*   **Non-Usage:** We do not use this data for advertising, user profiling, or any other non-security related purposes.

## 3. Data Safety & Retention

*   **Local Processing:** Most integrity analysis is performed locally on your device.
*   **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect "Personal Information" such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.
*   **Data Sharing:** We do not sell your technical data to third parties. Data is only shared with service providers (Google) as mentioned above to facilitate app functionality.
*   **Data Retention:** Since we do not store personal data on our servers, there is no retention of such data. Technical diagnostic data used by third-party services follows their respective retention policies.
